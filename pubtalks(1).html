  • My 24 Gresham lectures on the subjects of Maths and the Making of the Modern World.

    The talks cover the four areas of

    (i) Mathematical modelling (including chaos, climate, cocoa, cancer and saving the whales)
    (ii) Algorithms, good, bad, and mutant (including machine learning, Big Data, Page Rank, movies, CDs, GPS, and how maths can save your life)
    (iii) Maths and the human world (including mathematical cities, mathematical education, maths and art, maths and voting, and crowd behaviour)
    (iv) Maths in history (including 500 years of maths, mathematical myths, my favourite equations, and what have mathematicians done for us?)

    Each lecture has a video, a transcript and a power-point which you can download.
