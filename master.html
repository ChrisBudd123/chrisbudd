Royal Institution Masterclasses




This is a set of Royal Institution Masterclasses which I have developed for KS3 (for school students aged around 13). They are 2 1/2 hour workshops with a mixture of presentations and worksheets for two group working sessions. Please use these for your own classes.

  • Maths goes to the movies presentation and worksheet
  • Maths saves the whales presentation and worksheet
  • Maths of castles presentation and worksheet
  • What's the use of chaos presentation and worksheet
  • Dancing with maths presentation and worksheet
  • The mathematics of Celtic and African art (Celtic knots and Sona patterns) presentation and worksheet
  • The Maths of Google and the IPod presentation and worksheet
  • The maths of climate change presentation and worksheet
  • 101 uses of a quadratic equation presentation and worksheet 1 and worksheet 2
  • Magical maths presentation and worksheet
  • How to amaze your friends presentation and worksheet
  • Can maths help in the fight against crime? presentation and worksheet

Masterclasses for sixth form students

These are some presentations, worksheets and Python resources to allow Sixth Formers to explore some simple mathematical models of climate. They are part of the Climate-Pi project of Python based climate modelling tools.
  • Notes on models of climate change presentation 1 by myself, and presentation 2 by Dan Lunt (Bristol).
  • Climate change worksheet
  • Python tutorial
